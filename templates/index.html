Clinical Capture Guide — How to prevent false predictions
❌ Avoid Full Face / Teeth

Full face selfies, clenched teeth, lips, or clothing. The Swin model was trained strictly on intraoral mucosal tissue and will misclassify face skin as cancer.

✔️ Direct Intraoral Close-up

Open mouth wide. Hold camera 10–15 cm close, shining direct light on the specific mucosal tissue (tongue, cheek, gums, or lesion).
